About Cervical Cancer

Understanding the disease and the importance of early screening

Global Impact

Cervical cancer is the 4th most common cancer in women worldwide. Over 600,000 new cases are diagnosed annually, with the highest rates in low-income countries.

Preventable & Treatable

When detected early, cervical cancer has a 5-year survival rate above 90%. Regular Pap smears and HPV screening remain the gold standard for early detection.

HPV Connection

Nearly all cervical cancers are caused by persistent infection with high-risk HPV strains. Early identification of HPV-related cellular changes enables timely intervention.
